What happens

  1. Your wallet signs one plain transfer to a deposit key made in this browser.
  2. The deposit key puts it into Solana Privacy Rings, owned by a new note. You get the note.
  3. Later, from anywhere, the note pays any address. Zolana's relayer covers the network fee; 0.002 SOL stays behind as the fee.

The note is the money. Anyone holding it can unshield it, and nobody can recover it for you. Notes are kept in this browser too, under Notes.

Wait a while between shielding and unshielding, and avoid round amounts that match: timing and amounts are public.

HOW IT WORKS

Two addresses, one pool between them.

A t-address is an ordinary Solana wallet: public. A z-address is a note inside Solana Privacy Rings: its owner and balance are encrypted.

01 / t → z

Shield

SOL goes from your wallet into the pool. The chain shows a deposit and its size. It does not show which note owns it.

02 / z

Hold

The note sits in the pool with every other note. Nothing about it is readable without its key.

03 / z → t

Unshield

The note pays any address. The chain shows a payout from the pool, not from you.

LIMITS

What Zolana hides, and where it stops.

It hides

  • Which wallet a payout came from.
  • Who owns a note.
  • Any link from your wallet to the address you pay.

It does not hide

  • The size of a deposit or a payout.
  • When they happen.
  • Anything you do afterwards from a wallet people know is yours.

Who you rely on

  • Each note's key is derived by Zolana's server from a secret and the note. Its operator could move a note's balance.
  • Solana Privacy Rings, which Helius runs and calls alpha.
  • A pool is only as private as it is busy.
